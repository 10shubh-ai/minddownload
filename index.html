Install in three steps.

    Download & unzip

    Open MindDownload-Mac-arm64.zip and drag MindDownload.app into Applications.

    First launch

    The app isn't notarized yet. Right-click → Open, then confirm. You only need to do this once.

    Connect browsers

    In Settings, choose Connect browsers and load the bundled extension. Downloads now land in MindDownload.
